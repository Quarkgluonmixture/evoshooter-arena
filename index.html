how to read the arena
  • Capsules are agents; the dark visor shows facing, the translucent cone is what they can see.
  • Bar above the head = health. Ring on the ground = aim mode (slow, accurate).
  • Light on the head = what that player is saying on the radio right now. With a quantised radio each symbol has its own colour and silence is dark; with the old continuous radio it is a ramp. Random at first — if a symbol becomes stable and situational, signalling has evolved.
  • Zone in the middle scores 1 pt/s for the side with more agents inside. Wiping the enemy banks the remaining time.
  • Tall walls block sight; low walls hide legs and torso but not the head.
  • Camera: click a player tile or press 1–5 (red) / 6–0 (blue); Tab/Space next player; V 1st ↔ 3rd person; F free orbit; D auto-director follows the action. In 3rd person, drag to swing the camera around the player and scroll to pull back — a cut or a new player puts it back.
  • Panel: H hides or shows the side panel (evolution, behaviour, evidence, run).
